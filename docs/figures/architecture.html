Tested and set aside: masked pretraining. Hiding half of the surface and rebuilding it from the embedding works (error 0.16 against 1.04 for mean-fill) but gives no gain in temperature skill over five seeds, so the released model is trained end to end.
Supervised training. Masked mean-squared error on the anomaly, plus a small penalty on the vertical gradient between neighbouring depths. AdamW, cosine schedule, mixed precision, early stopping on the 2022 validation year.
About 20 minutes of training on eleven years of data on a 6 GB laptop GPU (RTX 3050), about 1.3 GB of GPU memory at batch size 8.src/oceanembed/models/
