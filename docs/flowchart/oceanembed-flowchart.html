OCEANEMBED

Subsurface ocean temperature, reconstructed from what satellites see at the surface

Satellite surface fields

one snapshot of the sea surface per day — seven harmonised, two used by the final model

SST · OSTIAsalinity · SMOS/SMAPsea level · DUACS currents U,V · OSCARwinds U,V · CCMP
At prediction time the model sees only what a satellite can see. Subsurface temperature is used to train and to score it, never as an input.

Harmonisation — one grid, one clock

0.25° × 0.25°, daily over the North Indian Ocean, 5–30°N · 45–105°E (100 × 240 cells)

Block-mean regridding0.05° and 0.125° products averaged to 0.25°, at least half the cells valid
Daily means6-hourly winds averaged to one value per day
Standard depthsGLORYS levels interpolated to 15 depths, 0 – 1 000 m

5 098 days, 2011-01-01 → 2024-12-15, no gaps

Currents and winds are subset on the server (OPeNDAP): a few hundred kilobytes per day instead of 33 MB global files, over fourteen years.
GATE

Split by time, never at random

train 2011 – 2021 · validate 2022 · test 2023 and 2024 — statistics come from the training years only

Neighbouring days look alike. A random split would leak the answer into training; a whole unseen year cannot.

Seasonal climatology removed

per cell and depth: mean + annual + semi-annual cycle — the model predicts only the anomaly

The same climatology is the no-skill baseline: every result is reported next to it, so a good seasonal cycle is never counted as skill.

Satellite embedding engine

CNN stem + Transformer encoder → 128 features on a 25 × 60 map of the basin

Two inputs are enoughSST and sea level, chosen on the validation year; salinity, currents and winds add nothing
Attention over the whole basin1 500 tokens: every patch can use eddies and fronts anywhere in the domain
Without sea level the error rises by 0.04 °C for this model and 0.27 °C for a per-pixel network; with SST alone, by 0.28 °C.

Reconstruction decoder

U-Net-style decoder with skip connections → temperature anomaly at 15 depths, then + climatology = °C

3.7 M parameters. Trains in about 20 minutes on a 6 GB laptop GPU; the loss ignores land and the sea floor.
